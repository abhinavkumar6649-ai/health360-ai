Mathematical Structure, Formula Weights & Clinical Limitations

Mathematical Basis This simulator computes a composite heuristic score [2%–95%] via bounded multi-factor weighting: logarithmic age scaling $\beta_{age} \cdot \max(0, \text{age}-30)$, JNC-8 hypertension thresholds (Systolic $\ge 130, 140, 160$ mmHg), ADA fasting glucose stratification ($\ge 100$ pre-diabetic, $\ge 126$ diabetic range), BMI classification penalties, smoking relative multiplier ($1.45\times$), and high-exercise cardioprotective discount ($0.72\times$).

Clinical Limitations This is not a validated medical prediction model. True clinical tools (such as the ACC/AHA ASCVD Risk Estimator Plus, Framingham Heart Study 10-Year Score, or UKPDS Risk Engine) require clinical biomarkers not present in this synthetic dataset—specifically HDL-C, treated vs. untreated antihypertensive status, race-specific hazard ratios, and baseline survival calibration $S_0(10)$.

Educational Notice: Output is intended exclusively for interactive population cohort exploration and risk communication demonstration. It does not provide medical diagnoses, treatment recommendations, or individualized clinical prognoses. Consult a licensed medical professional for medical guidance.
